   - **AWS Amplify**: Purpose-built frontend cloud, sub-second preview deployments, and zero container orchestration overhead over generic AWS credits.
   - **Cloudflare Pages**: Full Node.js runtime compatibility and dynamic ISR invalidation over V8 isolate limitations.
   - **DIY Kubernetes**: Zero-ops infrastructure, automatic multi-zone high availability, and massive reduction in platform engineering overhead.
3. **Trap-Setting Questions**: Formulate strategic discovery questions for Solutions Architects to expose incumbent limitations and steer architectural decisions toward Vercel-native capabilities.
