# Deal Qualification System

A lightweight, high-fidelity system for enterprise deal qualification that evaluates Account Executive and Solutions Architect notes against MEDDPICC criteria, generates dynamic technical qualification questions, and writes back suggested next steps into a simulated Salesforce CRM.

## Language

**Opportunity**:
A potential enterprise sales deal tracked within the simulated Salesforce CRM representing an engagement between Vercel and a prospect.
_Avoid_: Deal, lead, prospect record

**AE Notes**:
Initial qualitative notes recorded by an Account Executive capturing prospect business context, preliminary timelines, budget indicators, and competitor mentions.
_Avoid_: Sales notes, discovery log

**SA Notes**:
Technical discovery notes captured by a Solutions Architect detailing target architecture, frontend framework requirements, infrastructure constraints, and security standards.
_Avoid_: Tech notes, architect assessment

**Deal Stage**:
The current milestone of an Opportunity in the sales pipeline (e.g., Stage 2 - Discovery, Stage 3 - Technical Validation).
_Avoid_: Sales phase, opportunity status

**Suggested Next Steps**:
The single actionable writeback string generated by the qualification agent and persisted into the Opportunity record to guide field teams on the immediate next action.
_Avoid_: Action items, recommended steps, AI recommendations

**Qualification Status**:
The categorical state of an Opportunity's technical and commercial viability (Unqualified, In Review, Qualified, Disqualified).
_Avoid_: Deal score state, gate status

**MEDDPICC Score**:
A structured quantitative and qualitative evaluation across Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Paper Process, Identify Pain, Champion, and Competition.
_Avoid_: Opportunity rubric, sales scorecard

**Scenario**:
A deterministic, pre-canned enterprise customer profile used to seed the simulated CRM for demo and validation purposes.
_Avoid_: Mock deal, fixture, test case
