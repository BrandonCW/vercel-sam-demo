# Deal Qualification System

A lightweight, high-fidelity system for enterprise deal qualification that evaluates Account Executive and Solutions Architect notes against MEDDPICC criteria, generates dynamic technical qualification questions, and writes back suggested next steps into a simulated Salesforce CRM.

## Language

**Opportunity**:
A potential enterprise sales deal tracked within the simulated Salesforce CRM representing an engagement between Vercel and a prospect.
_Avoid_: Deal, lead, prospect record

**AE Notes**:
Initial qualitative notes recorded by an Account Executive capturing prospect business context, preliminary timelines, budget indicators, and competitor mentions.
_Avoid_: Sales notes, discovery log

**SA Notes**:
Technical discovery notes captured by a Solutions Architect detailing target architecture, frontend framework requirements, infrastructure constraints, and security standards.
_Avoid_: Tech notes, architect assessment

**Deal Stage**:
The current milestone of an Opportunity in the sales pipeline (e.g., Stage 2 - Discovery, Stage 3 - Technical Validation).
_Avoid_: Sales phase, opportunity status

**Suggested Next Steps**:
The single actionable writeback string generated by the qualification agent and persisted into the Opportunity record to guide field teams on the immediate next action.
_Avoid_: Action items, recommended steps, AI recommendations

**Qualification Status**:
The categorical state of an Opportunity's technical and commercial viability (Unqualified, In Review, Qualified, Disqualified).
_Avoid_: Deal score state, gate status

**MEDDPICC Score**:
A structured quantitative and qualitative evaluation across Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Paper Process, Identify Pain, Champion, and Competition.
_Avoid_: Opportunity rubric, sales scorecard

**Scenario**:
A deterministic, pre-canned enterprise customer profile used to seed the simulated CRM for demo and validation purposes.
_Avoid_: Mock deal, fixture, test case

**System 1**:
The fast rubric evaluation layer (powered by Jev via Vercel AI Gateway) that extracts structured MEDDPICC dimensions, detects competitors, and computes stage gate thresholds without deep reasoning overhead.
_Avoid_: Heuristic model, classifier, fast LLM

**Stage Gate**:
The formal criteria threshold required before an Opportunity can advance from its current Deal Stage to the next milestone in the sales process.
_Avoid_: Stage exit criteria, deal milestone gate

**System 2**:
The deep-reasoning LLM analysis layer that synthesizes qualification gaps, constructs targeted competitive playbooks, and formulates interactive questions for the Solutions Architect. Like System 1, it runs as a durable step of the `run_assessment` workflow tool; neither runs in a subagent.
_Avoid_: Reasoning engine, slow LLM, deep model

**JSON Render**:
The schema-driven dynamic UI rendering mechanism in Next.js that converts structured JSON component specifications into native interactive React form components.
_Avoid_: Dynamic form generator, declarative UI, JSON schema form

**Writeback**:
The atomic persistence operation that updates the Opportunity record in the simulated CRM with the agent's finalized Suggested Next Steps and qualification metrics.
_Avoid_: Sync back, database push, CRM export

**Delta Re-scoring**:
The rapid secondary evaluation executed by System 1 after an SA submits responses to dynamic qualification questions, measuring rubric score improvements and stage gate advancement.
_Avoid_: Re-evaluation, secondary grading, score update

**Assessment Session**:
The stateful, discrete evaluation lifecycle bounded between an initial assessment trigger and final CRM write-back, which pauses at zero compute cost while awaiting SA feedback. It is one durable eve session and one `run_assessment` workflow call, sequenced in code; the call's result (or its failure) is the session's verdict, decided in code.
_Avoid_: Evaluation run, qualification job, agent thread

## Architecture & Workbench Layout

### Deal Desk OS Split-Workbench Architecture
The Qualification Workbench adheres to a high-density, monochrome Deal Desk OS aesthetic with a 35% / 65% horizontal split layout (`DESIGN.md` "Warm Paper & Ink"):
- **Left Pane (35%) — Opportunity Brief (`DealContextCard`)**: Persistent opportunity context, 2x2 metadata grid (ACV, Close Date, Target Framework, Incumbent), competitor threat callouts, and collapsible narrative discovery notes with custom desktop scroll containers.
- **Right Pane (65%) — Stage Canvas Router**: High-density interactive canvas routing between blank start state, loading transitions, and the three qualification stages.
- **Quiet Monochrome Top Bar (`MonochromeTopBar`)**: High-contrast, minimal top bar providing Deal Desk branding, Opportunity name, sales pipeline stage, and formatted deal amount without colored badges or noise.

### Desk OS 3-Stage Lifecycle (`StepperBar`)
The progression bar routes across three canonical Desk OS stages:
1. **Stage 1 — Initial Deal Audit (`Initial Deal Audit (Gaps Identified)`)**: Baseline System 1 scoring and System 2 reasoning findings across all 8 MEDDPICC dimensions, highlighting blockers with recessed `#F1F0EC` row indicators and blocker counts.
2. **Stage 2 — Field Discovery Checklist (`Field Discovery Checklist (In-Context)`)**: In-context discovery form (`DynamicDiscoveryForm`) embedding live 10-segment bars (`TenSegmentBar`) in question headers, live `Answered {K} of {N}` progression, and manual SA observations.
3. **Stage 3 — Updated Assessment & Next Steps (`Updated Assessment & Next Steps (Qualified)`)**: Finalized deal posture displaying dynamic score deltas, Suggested Next Steps card with 1-click clipboard feedback, and full MEDDPICC ledger with staggered score animations.

### Non-Destructive Navigation & State Preservation
Stage switching via `StepperBar`, `← Back to Audit`, or `Cancel` is non-destructive. SA draft responses (`savedFormData`) and manual notes (`savedManualNotes`) are preserved in memory across stage changes until submitted or explicitly reset.
